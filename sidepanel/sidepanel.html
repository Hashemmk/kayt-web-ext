Kayt

      Keyboard shortcuts

      Add a note: Alt+N

      Voice note: Alt+V

      Start or end a range: Alt+R

      Listen to the video: Alt+L

      Snip: not set — set one at Change shortcuts

      In the note box, Alt+1 to Alt+4 save Important, Confusing, Revisit or Disagree.

      Kayt button on YouTube

      Voice and listening

      Voice notes, Snips and ranges turn speech into text on this computer. If "Keep audio" is on, a voice note also keeps a recording of your voice in this browser — never uploaded. The video's own sound is never recorded. Chrome needs a speech pack for your language, downloaded once.

      When off, press Stop or Alt+V to end a voice note.

      Notes: look back

      Notes point a little before the moment you press the shortcut, because you usually press it just after the part that mattered. Range ends are not moved.

      Ranges

      A range starts this many seconds before you press Start range (Alt+R). If Kayt was already listening, its transcript starts there too.

      Snip

      A Snip saves what the video said around the moment you press it: some seconds before, and some after, like the Snipd app. If Kayt wasn't listening yet, it can't hear the past, so it records the same total length starting from the press.

      Export

      Download every note as a Markdown file.

      Obsidian

      "Send to Obsidian" on a video's notes creates a note in your vault, in the same Markdown as the export. It goes straight to the Obsidian app on this computer; nothing is uploaded. Sending the same video again replaces its note there.

      Clear all data

      Permanently delete every note from this browser. This cannot be undone.

      Delete all notes? Export them first if you want to keep a copy.
