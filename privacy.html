Kayt for Chrome — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 September 2026. This describes version 1.3 of the extension.

In short: your notes — and, if you choose to keep them, audio recordings of your voice notes — stay on this computer, in this browser. Kayt has no server, no account, and no sync of any kind — including with the Kayt Android app, which is a separate product with its own storage.

Your notes stay on this computer

Notes are stored only in this browser's extension storage (chrome.storage.local), on this computer. If you keep "Keep audio" turned on in Settings, audio recordings are stored alongside them, also only on this computer, in this browser's local database (IndexedDB). Nothing is uploaded to any server. The extension has no account system and no cloud sync, and it does not sync with the Kayt Android app or any other device.

What the extension reads on YouTube

Kayt only runs on youtube.com and m.youtube.com. When you press a keyboard shortcut or click the Kayt button to add a note, it reads three things from the page at that moment: the video's ID (from the page address), the video's current playback position, and the name of the video's current chapter if it has chapters. It does not read anything else on the page, does not read other tabs, and does not read your browsing history.

Voice notes, Snip and listening to a video

Kayt can turn speech into text, and optionally keep a recording of it, in three ways, all only when you ask:

  • Voice notes use your microphone while you speak a note. The microphone is on only from the moment you start a voice note until it is saved, stopped or cancelled.
  • Listen to video listens to the sound of the one YouTube tab you turn it on for. A bar on the page and Chrome's own indicator show the whole time it is on. It stops when you turn it off, leave YouTube or close the tab.
  • Snip (previously called "Save what was said") saves what the video said just before and/or after the moment you pressed it, as a note. Starting a range also listens for the words said during it. If listening isn't already on when you use Snip or start a range, Kayt turns it on by itself for just that Snip or range, and turns it off again once it's done — you'll see this on that Snip's or range's own bar.

In all three cases the speech is recognised on this computer by Chrome's built-in on-device speech recognition; Kayt requires on-device mode and refuses to run if Chrome cannot guarantee it. Only recognised words are used to write a note's text.

If Settings' "Keep audio" is turned on (it is on by default), Kayt also keeps a short audio recording of your microphone alongside a voice note, covering the same stretch of time as the note. These recordings are stored only on this computer, in this browser's local database (IndexedDB) — never uploaded, never sent anywhere. They are deleted automatically when their note is deleted, or when you use "Clear all data", and you can turn "Keep audio" off at any time in Settings so future notes are saved without one.

The first time you use a language, Chrome downloads its speech pack from Google. That download is made by Chrome, contains no audio and no notes, and happens once per language.

What the extension fetches from the internet

The only network requests Kayt makes are to YouTube itself:

  • A lookup to YouTube's public oEmbed service to get a video's title and channel name, the first time you add a note to that video. This sends only the video's ID to YouTube.
  • Thumbnail images for videos in your notes list, loaded directly from YouTube's image servers (i.ytimg.com) when the side panel is shown.

Everything else the extension shows — its code, its icons and the two typefaces it is set in — is included in the extension itself, so no fonts or other files are fetched from Google or anyone else while you use it.

No other data is sent anywhere.

No tracking

No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no third-party tracking of any kind. Manifest V3 (the platform this extension is built on) also forbids extensions from downloading and running remote code — every script you're running shipped inside the extension package you installed.

Notes are not encrypted at rest

Unlike the Kayt Android app, which can use the phone's keystore to protect data, ordinary desktop browsers offer no equivalent secure storage that an extension can use. So notes are saved as plain data in your browser's local profile, not encrypted. Anyone with access to this computer's browser profile — for example another person using the same user account — could potentially read them. This is a deliberate, disclosed difference from the Android app, not an oversight.

Permissions this extension asks for

  • Storage — to save your notes locally, using chrome.storage.local.
  • Side panel — to show your notes list in Chrome's side panel.
  • Tab capture — to hear the sound of a YouTube tab when you turn on "Listen to video", use Snip, or start a range, so Kayt can turn what was said into text (and, if "Keep audio" is on, keep a short recording of it). Only the tab you choose, only while listening is on.
  • Offscreen document — a hidden Kayt page that does the speech recognition, because Chrome's background part of an extension cannot use a microphone or tab sound itself.
  • Microphone — asked by Chrome the first time you take a voice note, not at install. Used for voice notes, and, if "Keep audio" is on, to keep a recording of them alongside the note's text.
  • Access to youtube.com and m.youtube.com — so the extension can read the current video's ID and playback position when you ask it to, and show the note-capture box on the page.

Kayt does not request access to your other tabs, browsing history, or any site other than YouTube.

Your control

Every note can be exported at any time as a plain Markdown file (audio recordings are not included in the export). All data, including any saved audio recordings, can be permanently deleted from the extension's Settings ("Clear all data"), and deleting a single note deletes its recording too. Your data is also removed automatically if you uninstall the extension.

No sale or sharing of data

Kayt does not sell, rent, or share your notes or any other data with anyone, because it never leaves your browser in the first place — aside from the video ID sent to YouTube's oEmbed service described above.
